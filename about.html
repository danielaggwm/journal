What is this?

After filling countless Moleskines with scattered thoughts, I realized I never once flipped back to read them. Most my memories written, closed, and left to morph.

A friend once asked me, “If you could know one statistic about your life, what would it be?”

That question stuck with me. This site is my way of answering it — a personal dataset, a mood log, a trail of ordinary days. This is my record of being alive.
